# Privacy

Kept is local-first. Reminders, memories, chat history, learned patterns, and
preferences are stored on the user's Mac. Kept does not include analytics,
telemetry, advertising, accounts, or cloud synchronization.

Apple Foundation Models processes supported assistant requests on-device. Voice
dictation uses Apple's macOS Speech APIs and is subject to the capabilities and
settings provided by the operating system. Kept requests microphone and speech
recognition permission only for dictation.

Network access is used only to retrieve the configured Sparkle update feed and
signed update packages. The private development channel binds to `127.0.0.1`
and is not exposed to the local network.

Bug reports and screenshots can contain sensitive reminder content. Sanitize
them before sharing anything publicly.
