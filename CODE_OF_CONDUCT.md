# Code of Conduct

Kept contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of another person's private
information, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to
the maintainers rather than in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports
fairly, preserve confidentiality where possible, and avoid conflicts of interest.
